How one summer project taught an AI to audit other AIs. Six short scenes, about a minute in total. It plays on its own, or you can tap through at your own pace.

The problem

Great AI tools, checked by hand

AI tools were shipping faster and faster, but every output still had to be checked by a human auditor, one at a time. One audit cycle took about 2.5 weeks, and only a small sample of outputs ever got reviewed. Launches slowed down, and quality problems slipped through quietly.

The idea

Meet CheckMate

AI products rarely fail loudly. They fail quietly. CheckMate is the one measuring.

CheckMate flips the audit around. Instead of people sampling a few outputs by hand, a language model acts as the judge and reviews every single output against the same rubric the human auditors use.

Step 1 · Inputs

What goes in

The PM loads three things into CheckMate, and the judge takes it from there.

A batch of outputs

Everything the AI tool produced in the period being audited: drafts, replies, summaries, and more.

The rubric

The exact questions human auditors answer about each output, the same ones the judge will now answer.

Examples, optional

A few good and bad outputs, each with a short note explaining why, to anchor the judge's taste.

Step 2 · Judging

The judge gets to work

The judge model reads each output and answers every rubric question. It scores three dimensions, and it writes a rationale for every judgment, so nothing is a black box.

SAMPLE Fictional example case

OUTPUT FROM AN AI SHOPPING ASSISTANTGood news, your sneakers are on the way and will arrive by Friday. You can track them from the link in your shipping email.

Accuracy4/5
Completeness3/5
Tone5/5

Judge's rationale: the arrival day matches the order and the tone is warm, but the reply never gives the tracking link itself or mentions the return window, so Completeness loses points.

Design rule: a stronger model audits a weaker one Design rule: rubric-based prompts, not vibes
Step 3 · Outputs

What comes out

Every run produces three deliverables, ready for the team to act on.

Per-case audit report

One report for every output, mirroring the format human auditors produce, ready to review case by case.

Summary report

Overall scores for the whole batch, plus the key insights as plain bullet points a busy team can skim.

Drill-down lists

Every insight links to the cases behind it, grouped together so teams can run root cause analysis fast.

Results

It worked, and it spread

The machine audits matched human judgment, landed in days instead of weeks, and got picked up by other teams.
